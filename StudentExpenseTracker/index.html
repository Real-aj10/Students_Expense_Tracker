Verified Tactics
📖

Library Course Reserves

University libraries store physical copies of textbook syllabi behind circulation. Check them out for 2–4 hours and use scanner stations to save PDFs cleanly.

🚌

Subsidized Transit Stickers

Student union offices often offer semester bus/light-rail validation stickers for $10 or free. Validates unlimited city-wide rides without refilling transit cards.

🥫

Campus Food Pantries

Nearly all universities run discreet, zero-judgment student food banks stocked with fresh produce, protein bars, pasta, and emergency meal swipes.

🖨️

Department Free Printing

Skip commercial print shops: academic department computer labs (Engineering, English, Business) routinely include 250+ complimentary print pages per term.
